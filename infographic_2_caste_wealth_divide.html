In 2005 to 2006, just 11.2 percent of children in the poorest fifth held a certificate, against 57.3 percent in the richest. The gap has narrowed sharply since, but it has not closed.
Source: National Family Health Survey, NFHS-5 (2019 to 2021), Table 2.16, International Institute for Population Sciences and Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, full report via DHS Program archive (FR375). NFHS-3 (2005 to 2006) comparison figures, Table 2.19, IIPS. Figures measure certificate possession, which is consistently lower than registration: nationally 74.9 percent held a certificate against 89.1 percent registered.
