The Citizenship Act, 1955 names no single document as proof of citizenship. Aadhaar is available to any resident regardless of nationality. Foreign nationals earning Indian income hold PAN cards. The Ministry of External Affairs describes passports as travel documents issued on the presumption of citizenship, not as proof of it.
