Government-published registration levels 2020 to 2022, computed by Decode

The government did not publish a registration level for 2020, 2021 or 2022. The amber bars are Decode's own calculation from official registered birth counts, SRS crude birth rates and Census population projections, using the same formula the CRS itself defines. Applied to 2023 and 2024, where the real figures are known, this method reproduced them exactly. The 2020–22 outputs themselves have no independent government figure to check against.

Source: Civil Registration System Annual Reports 2009, 2019 and 2024, Office of the Registrar General of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, via dc.crsorgi.gov.in.
